Arrival is when guests form their first impression, and also when the most things can go wrong: the flight is delayed, nobody answers, details are missing. Pre-check-in takes that work out of arrival day and moves it to the days before, when there is time.

## What is worth asking for

- **Who is coming.** The name of the lead guest and of whoever travels with them. You know how many people to expect and avoid surprises with occupancy.
- **The expected arrival time.** This is the most useful piece of information for operations: it lets you organise the cleaning and whoever does the welcome.
- **The identification details the law requires.** In Portugal, anyone running holiday accommodation has to report foreign guests to the authorities, through SIBA. Collecting those details in advance saves time at the door. Check with the official bodies which details you must collect in your case.
- **Special requests.** A cot, a late arrival, allergies. Much better to know three days before.

## What is not worth asking for

Anything you are not going to use. Every extra field reduces the number of guests who complete the form, and every piece of personal data you keep is your responsibility. Ask for the minimum necessary and explain what it is for.

## When to send it

Too early, and the guest does not yet know what time they will arrive. Too late, and they are already travelling. A good moment is between a week and three days before arrival, with a reminder the day before for anyone who has not replied.

## How to make it easy for guests

- **One link, no accounts or installs.** The form should open on a phone and take two minutes.
- **Few steps.** First who is coming, then the arrival time. Nothing more.
- **Privacy information in plain view.** Say what data you collect, why and for how long.
- **Something in return.** After pre-check-in, send the arrival instructions. Guests see that filling it in did something for them.

## What the team gains

With arrival times known, cleaning is ordered by priority, whoever does the welcome is not left waiting and out-of-hours arrivals can be prepared in advance. In Santa Paciência, [online pre-check-in](/en/features/online-check-in/) is completed by the guest on their phone, with no accounts or apps, and the details land in the booking record.
